The rule this page follows

A prediction counts only if it was written before the measurement, so every prediction here carries its date. The measurement goes beside it, the deviation is arithmetic, and the explanation has to come from a foundation concept — bandwidth, switch resistance, tolerance or offset — not from the phrase “within tolerance”.

Eight of the thirteen quantities below have no measurement yet. They are listed as pending rather than left out, because a missing row is easy to mistake for a passing one.
