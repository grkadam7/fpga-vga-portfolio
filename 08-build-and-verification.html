Where the build stands

The analog chain is built and the gain network switches. The FPGA reads the pre-amp output and generates S1 S0. What is missing is the part that turns a working board into evidence: gain measured at each code, a frequency sweep, a switching transient, and every one of them written down.

Everything below separates what was measured from what is still a plan. A pending row is not a failure — it is a row that has not been earned yet.
