Open with the mentor

Two questions the team asked and has not had answered in writing.

  • Rail-to-rail requirement. The NE5532 is not rail-to-rail and the stage does not need it at 2.20 V peak, so the specification does not currently call for one. If the reviewer wants that as a requirement, it becomes a device-change decision rather than a wording change.
  • Distortion figure. THD is quoted as not applicable, because a DC input has no harmonic content to measure. If a figure is required anyway, it has to be measured with a test tone at the input, which is a different bench setup.

Readiness rule we work to

Three rules that decide whether a page is shown at a review.

  • A page shown at a review carries a date stamp earlier than the review date.
  • Content added during or after a review is welcome; it is recorded here as a response to feedback.
  • Every response links to the page where the change was made, so a reviewer can check it in one click.
