The rule for this page

Four people worked on this. Each writes their own reflection — the thing they got wrong, the concept that fixed it, and what they would do differently. One shared paragraph copied four times would tell a reviewer nothing, so the page stays empty until four separate voices fill it.

The table below tracks who owes what. A member's entries appear as their own block once written, not as a filled-in template.
