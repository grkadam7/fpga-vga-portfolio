The test this page has to pass

A course is named when it is mentioned, applied when the equation appears with our numbers in it, and verified when a measurement matches the prediction and the gap is explained. Nine decision cards carry the project. This page states which concept each one rests on and which level it has actually reached.

Nothing here is at Verified yet. Bench captures, not wording, are what move a card up — so every level below reads Applied until the measurements on page 9 exist.

Where two courses meet

The four places a decision cannot be explained by one course alone.

AEC × Signals & SystemsThe 24 dB dynamic range of the pre-amp output (20·log10(760/48.7)) is what sets how far the four gain levels must spread.
AEC × Network AnalysisThe 1 kΩ input resistor and the INA126's output impedance together decide whether Av = 1 + RF/Rin is the gain the board actually gives.
LIC × Signals & SystemsGBW sets the closed-loop time constant at each gain, and the settling budget is built on that constant.
AEC × DSDA code change re-routes RF inside the feedback path, so the FPGA must not move S1 S0 while a sample is being converted.

What is still open

Four entries are marked pending. Each one names what closes it.

  • DC-02 — input impedance. WS3 · C.2: the two topologies' input impedance, the gain sign, and what a 1 kΩ source demands. Closed by a worksheet entry, not by hardware.
  • DC-04 and DC-05 — RON and tolerance. WS4: where RON enters the gain expression, then the worst-case budget against ±5 %. Closed by calculation, checked against the measured gains.
  • DC-09 — settling. WS6 · D.2: dwell, switch tON, ΔV/SR and 4.6τ added up against 1 % in 50 µs. Closed by a scope capture of one gain step.
  • Every Verified level. The bench run on page 8 and the predicted-versus-measured table on page 9. Until those exist, this page reports Applied and says so.
