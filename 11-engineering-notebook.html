What an entry has to contain

A date, an objective, the decision or calculation that followed, the course it came from, the learning, and the next action. If an entry has no learning, it is a diary page rather than a record — five rows below still say pending for exactly that reason.

Week numbers are fixed; the calendar dates are still to be filled in from the team's log. Every entry here was written before the measurement it refers to, which is the only reason the predictions on page 9 can be judged at all.

Week 2 — how many gains, and how many bits to choose them.

ObjectiveDerive the required gain range and the digital control interface from the input characterisation.

Course usedAnalog Electronic Circuits — closed-loop gain; Signals & Systems — dynamic range.

Entry 4 · Op-amp and configuration Date pending

Week 3 — four resistors, one part, and the numbers that chose them.

ObjectiveSelect the gain-stage op-amp from the comparison and compute RF for each gain.
Decision / calculationNon-inverting, Av = 1 + RF/Rin, Rin = 1 kΩ, RF = 1 k / 2.2 k / 4.7 k / 10 kΩ, device NE5532. Its 1.1 MHz closed-loop bandwidth at ×11 is three orders of magnitude beyond a load-cell signal.
Course usedAnalog Electronic Circuits — non-inverting gain; Linear Integrated Circuits — GBW, slew rate, offset.

Entry 5 · Simulation of the analog chain Date pending

Weeks 4 and 5 — the first numbers that were not theory.

ObjectiveCheck the four gain settings and the clipping behaviour before anything was soldered.
Decision / calculationLTspice model: load cell source → INA126 → NE5532 stage with CD4052-switched RF, ±5 V rails, ideal switches. VIN driven over the measured range. The XADC is not in the model: it samples VIN, it does not see the VGA output.
Course usedAnalog Electronic Circuits — feedback and clipping; Digital System Design — code-to-channel mapping.
LearningSimulated 1.9 / 3.1 / 5.6 / 10.8 against theory 2.0 / 3.2 / 5.7 / 11.0. The shortfall is roughly a constant 0.1 in gain rather than a constant percentage, so it behaves like an additive error — an open item on page 10.
Next actionBench-measure the same four codes and compare with the simulation (Worksheet 6).

Housekeeping

How the notebook stays a record rather than a reconstruction.

  • One entry every Friday, dated that Friday. An entry written after the fact is labelled as such.
  • Each entry closes with what was learned and what was verified, in that order.
  • Every entry names the worksheet it closed out, so the worksheets and the notebook cannot drift apart.
  • The home page progress strip is updated the same day, not at the end of the sprint.
