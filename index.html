Sprint 1 · Build & Verification

The circuit works; the paperwork is catching up

Last updated 2026-10-27

Hardware is ahead of the documents: the full chain — load cell → pre-amp → XADC → FPGA → mux → VGA → output — is built and switching gains on the bench. Three of nine worksheets are filled and the FR table is still in draft.

✓On the bench (done)

  • Load cell characterized: 12 points — 0.1 mV at 0 kg → 4.7 mV at 5 kg, bridge ≈1 kΩ
  • INA126 pre-amp measured: 48.7 mV unloaded → 760 mV at 5 kg (gain ≈ ×165)
  • NE5532 VGA + CD4052 mux switching all four gains under FPGA control
  • LTspice model runs at all four gain codes

◐In progress

  • Documentation catch-up: 3 of 9 worksheets filled
  • FR table drafted — freezes at Review 3
  • Waveform captures and labelled evidence uploads

→Next up

  • Hold the VGA output at a constant 2 V
  • Drive a motor from the VGA + FPGA
  • Post sensor-characterization and pre-amp gain data
